Running the lights

For whoever's at the laptop at Revel: put the show on once, then open it and press play. Tick each step as you go.

Put the show on the laptop

Do this once, on the laptop at Revel (the one with ShowXpress on it), before playing anything.

  1. On that laptop, open the lighting page, scroll down past the stage map, and click Download for ShowXpress (in the row of buttons under Cues).It saves teen-titans-showxpress.zip in Downloads. The button isn't there on a phone.
  2. Open the Downloads folder. If there's already a folder called teen-titans-showxpress, drag it to the Bin. Then double-click the zip.You now have a teen-titans-showxpress folder.
  3. If ShowXpress is open, quit it: click it, then press Cmd + Q.
  4. Open Terminal: press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Enter.
  5. Click Copy, click in the Terminal window, paste with Cmd + V, and press Enter. 
    bash ~/Downloads/teen-titans-showxpress/install.sh
    After a few seconds it says Done. If it says ShowXpress is open, quit it (step 3) and paste again.

These steps are for a Mac. If the laptop is Windows, stop here and tell Lisa.

Get it going

  1. Plug the lights' USB box (the DMX adapter) into the laptop.
  2. Open ShowXpress.It's in the Dock, or in Applications › ShowXpress.
  3. Top menu: File › Open lightshow › Teen Titans.The top of the window should now say "ShowXpress - Teen Titans".
  4. Click Live at the top, then TimeLine.
  5. Top right, find the small DMX icon. It needs a green line under it. If the line is red, click the icon once.This is what sends the show to the real lights.

Play the show

  1. Click the folder icon (Open) at the top of the TimeLine and pick Teen Titans.
  2. Press ▶ play.The first 5 seconds are dark on purpose, then the music and lights start together. The clock at the top left counts up, 5 seconds ahead of the song.
  3. To stop, press ■ stop.

Starting from a part of the song

Double-click the time ruler above the coloured blocks, near the clock time below. A red marker appears there. Then press Play from red marker (the button next to play).

ClockPart of the song

Starting partway, the lights can be off for a moment until the next change. If it looks wrong right after starting, start a few seconds earlier.

Questions, or something looks off? Text Lisa the clock time and what you saw.

Quick fixes

The music plays but the lights don't.Check the green line under the DMX icon (top right). Still nothing? Click Editor at the top, then Steps: if the DMX or 3D buttons there are green, click them off. Then go back to Live › TimeLine.
The lights lag behind the music.Press stop, then play again from the start. It sometimes lags the first time through.
Teen Titans isn't in the list.Check the top of the window says "ShowXpress - Teen Titans". If not, File › Open lightshow › Teen Titans.
When you're done.Press stop, then quit with Cmd + Q.
